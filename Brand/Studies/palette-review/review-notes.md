# palette comparison

this round holds the selected compact-orbit geometry and the selected 0.7 px gap at 16 px constant. only the direction-tip color changes. the monochrome reference uses black tips on light surfaces and white tips on dark surfaces. three warm-orange samples are provisional: `#C44E19`, `#D45D21`, and `#E16B2A`.

## contrast and appearance tradeoffs

| treatment | against white | against black | tradeoff |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| monochrome reference | 21.00:1 (black) | 21.00:1 (white) | strongest adaptable contrast; preserves the quiet monochrome direction but has no warm accent. |
| warm orange 01 · provisional `#C44E19` | 4.72:1 | 4.45:1 | darkest sample; strongest white-surface contrast, but least separation from black in dark appearance. |
| warm orange 02 · provisional `#D45D21` | 3.90:1 | 5.39:1 | middle sample; balances warm character with dark-surface visibility; remains a small accent. |
| warm orange 03 · provisional `#E16B2A` | 3.31:1 | 6.34:1 | brightest sample; strongest black-surface contrast, but weakest white-surface contrast. |

ratios describe flat sRGB colors against pure black and white. they do not predict actual menu-bar contrast: macOS materials can be translucent, tinted by the desktop, and affected by increased-contrast settings, display scaling, and antialiasing. the orange appears only on small direction tips while the frame and loop adapt between black and white. inspect the actual menu bar under light, dark, and increased-contrast appearances before choosing a value.

each preview has alternative text that identifies its palette, appearance, surface, and size; headings also expose the same comparison context. color is not the only label.
