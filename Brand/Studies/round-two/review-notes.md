# round-two visual tradeoffs

study c from round one was selected for refinement. all three variants keep its vertical-loop direction inside the same crop frame. these are monochrome review studies, not approved artwork.

- **variant a · clean arcs:** no arrowheads; simplest and clearest at 16 px, with a quieter motion cue.
- **variant b · directional ends:** clearer motion when enlarged; small tips may crowd at 16 px.
- **variant c · offset return:** adds movement with asymmetry; may feel less calm.

architect choices still open: which silhouette proceeds, whether directional tips or offset belong, whether the frame survives at menu-bar size, and later orange values, motion, sound, and usage rules. nothing is chosen here beyond the round-one selection of the vertical-loop direction.
