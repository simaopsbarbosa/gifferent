# compact-loop final-art review

this review refines selected treatment 02, breathing room. it preserves the compact vertical loop and crop frame. each direction tip is separated from the nearest curve by a measured outline gap: approximately 5.4 svg units at the closest centerline point, or about 1.36 px at a 16 px render. round caps and unchanged opposing curves help the gap read as designed rather than a broken stroke.

## swatches and contrast

the monochrome option keeps frame, curves, and tips black. each provisional orange colors only the direction tips; the orange is a separate svg group and can be removed without changing the geometry.

| option | sample | contrast on white | contrast on black |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| burnt orange | `#C44E19` | 4.72:1 | 4.45:1 |
| warm orange | `#D45D21` | 3.90:1 | 5.39:1 |
| bright orange | `#E16B2A` | 3.31:1 | 6.34:1 |

ratios compare the flat swatch against pure white and pure black. the high-contrast sheet alternates those surfaces by size. ratios do not assess antialiased edge pixels, nearby content, or final macOS materials; test the mark against actual light and dark appearances before setting usage rules. all three orange values are provisional examples only.
