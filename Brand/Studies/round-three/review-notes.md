# round-three visual tradeoffs

these are refinements of approved direction variant b: directional vertical-loop arcs within an open crop frame. each study is shown in monochrome and with a provisional warm-orange accent. orange values are samples only; no palette is selected.

- **01 · integrated ends:** closest to the approved geometry and most open at 16 px; its motion cue may be subtle.
- **02 · flowing return:** smoother curves make the loop feel more fluid when enlarged; the inner gap may soften when small.
- **03 · compact orbit:** more negative space around the frame; the path may read as two separate marks.

architect choices remain open: final silhouette, arrowhead treatment, exact orange values and contrast rules, and whether the mark receives motion or optional sound. none of these studies is final or approved artwork.
