approved quality floor

  • text remains legible at native size.
  • cursor edges stay clear.
  • gradients show no distracting banding or flicker.
  • minor differences visible only at 2× are acceptable.

static snapshots support checking text, cursor boundaries, and spatial gradient artifacts. they cannot establish temporal flicker or full-animation quality. see the README for inspection notes and provenance.
